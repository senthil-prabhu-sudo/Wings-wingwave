## Overview

**WingsPlayer** is a media player for Android designed for offline and online audio and video playback. Built with Flutter, it combines media playback, on-device audio/video trimming tools, link conversion, and a privacy vault into a single interface.

---

## Features

### 🎵 Audio Player
- Plays standard audio formats (MP3, AAC, FLAC, WAV, M4A, OGG).
- Background playback with notification and lock-screen controls.
- Interactive multi-band equalizer with presets.
- Queue management with shuffle, repeat, and sleep timer.

### 🎬 Video Player
- Video playback powered by `media_kit` (libmpv).
- Picture-in-Picture (PiP) support.
- Swipe gestures for volume, brightness, and seeking.
- Subtitle selection and audio track switching.
- Variable playback speeds (0.25x – 2.0x).

### 🛠️ Media Tools
- **Audio Extractor**: Extract audio tracks from video files on-device.
- **Audio Trimmer**: Trim audio files for custom clips or ringtones.
- **Video Trimmer**: Trim and export video segments.
- **Link Converter**: Convert and download online media links directly.
- **Exports Manager**: View, play, and share exported media files.

### 🔒 Privacy Vault
- Password-protected storage for private media files.

### 🌐 Online Music
- Search and stream online tracks with metadata and artwork caching.
