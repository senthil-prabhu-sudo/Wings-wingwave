## 📖 Overview

**WingWave** is a full-featured media player for Android built with Flutter. It combines high-performance audio and video playback, on-device media editing tools, online music streaming, and a privacy vault into a clean, modern interface.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎵 Audio Player
- **Format Support**: Plays MP3, AAC, FLAC, WAV, M4A, OGG, and other standard formats.
- **Background Playback**: Continues playing when the app is minimized or screen is locked, with system notification and lock-screen controls.
- **Audio Equalizer**: Interactive multi-band equalizer with custom presets and audio enhancement.
- **Playback Controls**: Queue management, shuffle, repeat modes, and a customizable sleep timer.

### 🎬 Video Player
- **Hardware Acceleration**: Video playback powered by `media_kit` (libmpv).
- **Picture-in-Picture (PiP)**: Keep videos playing in a floating window while using other apps.
- **Gesture Controls**: Swipe vertically for brightness and volume; swipe horizontally to seek.
- **Tracks & Subtitles**: Multi-audio track switching and external/embedded subtitle selection.
- **Speed Control**: Variable playback speed from 0.25x to 2.0x.

### 🛠️ Built-in Media Tools
- **Audio Extractor**: Extract audio tracks directly from video files on your device.
- **Audio Trimmer**: Trim audio files with precision for custom cuts and ringtones.
- **Video Trimmer**: Lossless video trimming and exporting.
- **Link Converter**: Convert and stream/download online media links directly.
- **Exports Manager**: Easily view, manage, and share your exported files.

### 🔒 Privacy Vault
- Password-protected vault to hide and secure private audio and video files away from your device gallery.

### 🌐 Online Music Streaming
- Search and stream online tracks with metadata and album artwork caching.
- Built-in stream proxy support for uninterrupted playback.

### 🎨 User Interface & Customization
- Modern Material Design 3 interface with dark and light theme support.
- Organize media by songs, videos, folders, artists, and custom playlists.
- Fast, instant search and sorting across your entire media library.
